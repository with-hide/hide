Hide for iPhone is the phone side of Hide, a free desktop program that people run on their own Mac, Windows or Linux computer (withhide.dev). The app has no account, so there is no demo account. Its built-in demo with sample data opens every screen and shows the full feature set; we ask that it be the way the app is reviewed (guideline 2.1(a)).

HOW TO TRY THE APP WITHOUT A COMPUTER
1. Open the app and tap "Try the demo" on the first screen ("체험해 보기" in Korean).
2. A slim bar, "Demo · sample data", stays on top of every screen. Nothing goes to a computer and nothing from the demo is kept (only language, appearance and the iOS permission persist).
3. Agents tab: the sample computer's agents, grouped Needs You, Working, Unread and Seen. Open one to read and reply; the demo answers with a sample reply. "Start agent" adds one.
4. Missions tab: a mission with its steps, a pull request per step and one decision to answer.
5. PRs tab: sample pull requests with status and reviews. "Merge" takes one off the list; "Assign to an agent" hands it to a sample agent.
6. In the Agents tab open the second agent under Needs You, "Tidy the install guide", and its "To look at" tab: one page made by an agent ("Install guide") and one image, shown in the app.
7. Settings tab: language, and Notifications. Notifications are optional and off by default; iOS asks only when the user turns the switch on, and the app works fully without them. Turn it on and allow. About 2 seconds later a sample notification arrives, worded like a real one: "Address review comments", "Question · Apply all 3 review comments? · demo · fix/review", with a Reply action. Lock the phone first to see it on the lock screen.
8. "Leave demo" returns to the first screen. The host sheet (tap "Demo Mac" in the list's header) has "Connect a real computer": the QR scanner (camera permission); pairing needs a real computer.

WHY THIS IS MORE THAN A WEB PAGE (4.2)
- Encrypted push: a notification service extension in the app decrypts Apple's push on the device, so neither Apple nor our relay sees the text.
- Reply from the notification: the action needs the phone unlocked (Face ID or passcode) and sends the reply end-to-end encrypted to the user's computer.
- Pairing keys are kept in the iOS Keychain.
- The app connects only to the relay and to Apple's push service, and has no analytics or ad tools.
- A native QR scanner pairs the phone with a computer.
- Universal links (app.withhide.dev) open a pairing link in the app.

4.2.3 AND 4.2.7
Commands and agents run on the user's computer, not on the phone. The app downloads no code that changes its features; it shows agents, conversations, missions, pull requests and results in its own interface and sends the user's typed input back. The one exception is a page the user's own agent made, which "To look at" draws (see below). The connection goes through a relay and is end-to-end encrypted (Noise).

"TO LOOK AT" (agent-made HTML)
The "To look at" tab can show a page that the user's own agent made on the user's own computer. The page, with its scripts, is drawn inside a sandboxed frame with its own origin, so it cannot reach the app, its storage or its connection. It is the user's own content, not third-party software, and does not change the app's features (2.5.2; 4.7 does not apply).

ENCRYPTION
The app uses only standard encryption (Noise_NK with X25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305 and BLAKE2b for the connection; Ed25519 signatures; RFC 8291 with P-256, AES-128-GCM and HMAC-SHA-256 for notifications) for its own connection and notifications only, so it is exempt.
