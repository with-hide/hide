# Age rating answers

The answers for App Store Connect's age-rating questionnaire (App Information > Age Rating), with the reason for each.
The expected result is 4+.
Apple changes the wording and the list of questions; the reasons below are the stable part, so read each question for what it asks before ticking the answer.

What the app is, for every answer: the phone side of a program the person runs on their own computer.
It shows the person's own coding agents, their conversations, missions and pull requests, and sends back what the person types.
It has no account, no ads, no purchases, no feed and no way to reach another person.

## In-app controls and capabilities

| Question | Answer | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unrestricted web access | No | The app has no browser. Its one web view shows the app's own pages and, on the "To look at" tab, a page the person's own agent made on their own computer, in a sandboxed frame; the app follows no link into the web. |
| User-generated content | No | Nothing a person writes reaches another person. The conversations are between the person and their own agent. |
| Messaging and chat | No | The person types to their own agent on their own computer; there is no other person in the conversation and no chat between users. |

## Notes for the reviewer of the answers

- The text of an agent's conversation is produced by the person's own agent on the person's own computer, and the app shows it as it came.
  Answers that depend on whether the app "generates" content use that fact: the app does not generate it.
- The app is distributed without the EU in this release, so the EU-only questions (for example the Digital Services Act's trader status) are the account holder's to answer in App Store Connect.
