Orca notification-status helper
===============================

Copyright (c) 2026 Lovecast Inc.

The macOS app ships a notification permission helper
(hide-notification-status, in Contents/MacOS) compiled from
desktop/native/notification-status/main.swift. That file, and the way
desktop/scripts/package.mjs compiles it with the app's identifier embedded as
its Info.plist, are derived from Orca's
native/notification-status-macos/main.swift and
config/scripts/build-notification-status-macos.mjs
(https://github.com/stablyai/orca, compared at commit
20b28609f4032a6b81276959b17051d8a6fb4a04). Hide changed them: main.swift says
what it took and what changed.
