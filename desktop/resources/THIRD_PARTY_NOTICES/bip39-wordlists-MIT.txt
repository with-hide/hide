BIP-39 word lists
=================

Copyright (c) 2013 Pavol Rusnak
Copyright (c) 2017 mruddy

Hide shows four confirmation words when two machines are paired. The words
come from the English and Korean word lists of BIP-39, as
trezor/python-mnemonic ships them in src/mnemonic/wordlist
(https://github.com/trezor/python-mnemonic): hide-peer/src/words/en.txt is
its english.txt unchanged, and hide-peer/src/words/ko.txt is its korean.txt
stored in Unicode NFC, with the same words in the same order.
