Questions

Before you install.

Is hide a wrapper around my agent?

No. hide adds its hooks to each agent's own settings and a skill the agent can call, and the vendor's CLI runs as it does without hide. Your subscription, config, skills and MCP servers stay as they are.

Which agents work with it?

Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, OpenCode, Grok, Pi and omp, each with its own login. Every one gets the same status, title and place to answer. Grok takes messages only while it works and does not use Project Memory yet.

How do I connect my phone?

On macOS, Windows or Linux, turn on Mobile in Settings and scan the QR with your phone camera. Open app.withhide.dev and connect, with no Hide account, payment or Tailscale installation. Keep your computer awake and connected. You can add the phone app to your Home Screen, enable notifications and switch between paired computers. Tailscale remains an advanced direct option where supported.

Can the relay read my conversations?

No. Your phone and computer encrypt their messages before sending them through Hide's relay in Tokyo. The relay forwards them and cannot read conversations, commands or phone credentials. It can see connection times, traffic sizes and the routing identifiers that join your devices. Agents, the core and Hide AI still run on machines you own, using the agent CLIs you already signed in to; those CLIs send data to their providers as before.
