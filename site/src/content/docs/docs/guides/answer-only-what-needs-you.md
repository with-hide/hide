**Use when** several agents are working and you do not want to keep checking each terminal.

<iframe src="/embed/docs-embed.html?part=task-row&theme=dark" title="A task row moving from working to your turn to done" height="140" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Yellow means it is your turn. Everything else can wait.

## Steps

1. Start your agents as usual. Press `⌘N` for each task.
2. Work on something else. Rows that need no one stay quiet.
3. When a row turns yellow, open it. The card shows the question and the choices.
4. Pick an answer. The agent continues, and the row goes back to working.

## What it is made of

Read how a row works in [Tasks and status](/docs/features/tasks-status/). To keep the answer for next time, see [Project Memory](/docs/features/project-memory/).
